About the Windy Ridge Site

Where Deep Thinking Meets
Purposeful Action

Our Windy Ridge campus is the primary seat of Palena Gardens' consultancy practice. Set within a quiet, campus-like estate on Broadleaf Street, it offers an environment deliberately designed to slow down the noise of organizational life — creating the conditions for genuine insight and transformation.

Led by Augustine Sagoe, a practitioner with over 20 years of experience at the intersection of organizational psychology, group relations, and executive development, our team brings rigorous frameworks and deep human sensitivity to every engagement.

Whether you bring us a persistent team conflict, a strategic inflection point, or the quiet erosion of organisational culture — we meet you where you are.
