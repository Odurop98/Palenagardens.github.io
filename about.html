Palena Gardens was founded on the conviction that leadership, wellbeing, and cultural enrichment are inseparable — and that the spaces we inhabit shape the people we become.

Our Foundation

A Holistic Vision

Palena Gardens was founded on the conviction that leadership, wellbeing, and cultural enrichment are inseparable. Our two sites — one dedicated to professional development, the other to gracious hospitality — reflect this holistic vision.

From our Windy Ridge campus where executives engage in deep consultancy work, to our Anaji site where guests experience warm Ghanaian hospitality, every corner of Palena Gardens is designed with intention and care.

We believe the most effective leaders are those who are well — in mind, in body, and in their sense of cultural identity. This belief animates everything we do.

"Leadership, wellbeing, and cultural enrichment are inseparable."

— The Palena Gardens Mission

Leadership

Our Directors

Guided by decades of combined experience in organisational psychology, hospitality, and leadership development.

AS

Principal Consultant & Director

Augustine Paapa Sagoe

Augustine Paapa Sagoe is the founding principal of Palena Gardens. With decades of experience in organizational consultancy and group relations, he brings systems psychodynamic insight to leaders and institutions across West Africa and internationally.

His work centres on enabling leaders to navigate complexity, understand unconscious organizational dynamics, and foster cultures of reflective practice. He is widely regarded as one of Ghana's foremost practitioners in the field of Group Relations.

HS

Hospitality Lead & Director

Helena Sagoe

Helena Sagoe oversees the hospitality operations at Palena Gardens, ensuring that every guest experiences the highest standards of comfort and care.

Her leadership at the Anaji site has established it as a trusted name in Takoradi's hospitality landscape, known for reliability, warmth, and an unwavering attention to detail that transforms a stay into a genuine experience of Ghanaian welcome.
